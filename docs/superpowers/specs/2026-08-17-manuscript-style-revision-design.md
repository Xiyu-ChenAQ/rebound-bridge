# Manuscript Style Revision Design

## Objective

Make the MNRAS manuscript more direct and easier to follow without changing its scientific content, evidence, numerical values, equations, citations, or claim strength.

## Scope

The revision will:

- Reduce the manuscript's 16 uses of `while` to approximately 4--6.
- Split compound contrast sentences when two independent claims are easier to read separately.
- Replace conditional uses of `while` with precise alternatives such as `when` or `provided that`.
- Retain `while` only for genuine simultaneity or a concise, necessary contrast.
- Remove nearby duplicated defensive qualifications when the same limitation is already stated clearly elsewhere.
- Preserve the established MNRAS register and use Rein (2020) only as a directional model for directness and stable terminology.

## Out Of Scope

- No full-manuscript rewrite.
- No changes to formulas, numerical results, experimental settings, figures, bibliography, or citations.
- No strengthening or weakening of scientific claims.
- No new terminology, sections, experiments, or literature claims.
- No removal of unique limitations needed to delimit validity.

## Terminology Rules

Prefer the following terms consistently:

- `main simulation`
- `subsystem simulation`
- `subsystem monopole`
- `residual interaction B` for the Hamiltonian interaction
- `residual kick` for the flow generated by `B`
- `outer step h`
- `inner step delta`

Use `tidal` only when describing the physical weak-coupling limit. Do not mechanically replace technically distinct terms such as Hamiltonian, interaction, force, flow, and kick.

## Revision Priorities

1. Remove clause stacking caused by `while` throughout the manuscript.
2. Simplify the comparison language in the validation section.
3. Consolidate repeated qualifications in the exomoon application and conclusion.
4. Lightly simplify abstract or contribution prose only where the same edit is necessary for directness.

## Verification

- Review every remaining occurrence of `while` in context.
- Compare equations, numbers, citations, labels, and figure paths before and after revision.
- Compile with the local MNRAS v3.2 class using Tectonic.
- Confirm that there are no undefined citations or references.
- Visually inspect the first page and pages affected by pagination changes.
